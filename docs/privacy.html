Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026 · applies to Insomniac for macOS, both the direct download and the Mac App Store version.

Insomniac collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is recorded or transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings — auto-off length, thermal and battery cutoff levels, and the other preferences — are stored locally on your Mac using the standard macOS preferences system. They never leave the device, and they are removed when you delete the app.

The one network request

Insomniac makes network requests only when you turn on the optional Use local weather setting, which folds the outdoor temperature into its advice about how long a session is safe. With that setting on:

  • Your device's public IP address is sent to an IP-geolocation service (GeoJS, falling back to freeipapi.com) to estimate roughly which city you are in. This is not GPS, it asks for no Location Services permission, and it is accurate only to city level.
  • Those approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the current temperature.

Nothing else is sent, nothing is attached to an identity, and no result is stored. Turn Use local weather off and Insomniac makes no network requests at all — the safety advisory still works, using your Mac's thermal state, power source, and system load.

Those three services are independent of Insomniac and have their own privacy practices, linked above.

Updates (direct download only)

The version downloaded from this site checks for new releases using Sparkle. That check is a request for an update file hosted on GitHub; it sends no information about you or your Mac beyond what any ordinary web request necessarily reveals, and it does not identify you. You can turn it off in Settings → General → Automatic updates. The Mac App Store version does not include this — the App Store handles its updates.

What Insomniac reads on your Mac

To do its job the app reads system state: thermal level, power source and battery percentage, whether the lid is closed, whether an external display is attached, and which apps are currently holding your Mac awake. All of this is read through Apple's standard APIs, used only to show you what's happening and to run the safety cutoffs, and none of it is transmitted or stored.

Children

Insomniac is a utility with no user-generated content, no messaging, and no data collection, so there is nothing to collect from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date. Given the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: open an issue at github.com/Saif-09/insomniac.

Insomniac is open source. If you would rather verify than trust, the code is all there — the weather requests are in Weather/, and there is no analytics SDK anywhere in the project.
